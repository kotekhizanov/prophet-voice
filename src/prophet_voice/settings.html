Prophet Voice

Talk to Prophet aloud. Say the wake word, then your question; Prophet answers in its voice. After saving, restart prophet-voice run. Once the voice is a plugin, the chat, the voice, the threshold and Whisper are set in Omniscient (Settings → Plugins → Voice → Settings), and those win over this page.
